# My Favorite Movie: F1 🏎️

**F1** (Formula 1) is my favorite movie because it’s full of speed, competition, and teamwork.  
I really enjoy watching the drivers race and how they push their limits on every track.

## What I Like About F1
- The sound of the cars and the excitement of the race.  
- The strategy behind every pit stop.  
- The amazing technology used in each car.  
- The passion and dedication of the drivers.
